Open the **Create** tab to write a post. A post is built from parts, which you can arrange in any
order.

## Parts of a post

- **Paragraph:** a block of text.
- **Photo:** JPEG, PNG or WebP.
- **Video:** MP4 or MOV.

Add parts with the buttons below the editor, and drag them to change the order. A post needs at
least one paragraph, photo or video.

## Photo and video shapes

So that every post fits the feed the same way, photos and videos come in two shapes:

- **Upright** (4:5)
- **Wide** (4:3)

When you add a **photo**, you crop it to one of the two shapes right there. What you see in the
crop is what readers see.

A **video** can't be cropped in the browser. Instead, you choose a shape and the video is trimmed
to it from the middle of the picture when you publish. The preview shows what will be cut off.

## Privacy of your files

Photos and videos are re-encoded when you publish, and their hidden metadata is removed along the
way. That includes the location where a photo was taken and the device it was taken with.

## Channel and language

Every post needs:

- a **channel**: where it's published. It only travels among that channel's members.
- a **language**: the language your post is written in. Only people who read that language will
  receive it. The app suggests a language based on your text, marked *Suggested*.

**No language** is only for posts without any text: a photo or a clip. They reach everyone in the
channel, whatever language they read. As soon as your post contains text, pick the language you
wrote it in.

## Posting anonymously

You can publish a post **anonymously**. Other people then don't see your name or profile picture
on it.

> Anonymous doesn't mean untraceable. On our side the post is still linked to your account, and
> the [community guidelines](/docs/guidelines/) apply just the same.

## Preview

**Preview** shows your post exactly as readers will see it. Use it: once it's published, a post
can't be edited or deleted on its own.

## Limits

| | Limit |
|---|---|
| Photos and videos per post | 5 |
| All files together | 40 MB |
| One photo | 12 MB |
| One video | 25 MB and 60 seconds |
| Parts per post | 40 |
| One paragraph | 10,000 characters |

## Publishing

**Publish** spends the price shown and sends your post on its way. What happens next is described
in [After you publish](/docs/after-publishing/).

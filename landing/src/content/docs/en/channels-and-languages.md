Two settings decide what can reach your feed: the channels you've joined and the languages you
read. Both are in the **Filters** tab.

## Channels

A channel is a topic, for example *General*, *Technology*, *Outdoors*, *Memes*, *Politics* or
*Local*. Every post belongs to exactly one channel and only travels among its members.

- **Join** a channel to receive its posts, **leave** it to stop.
- There's no limit on how many you join. More channels, fuller feed.
- Channels are set up by the Peerkola team. You can't create your own yet.
- **Channel costs** shows what posting costs in each channel. It's off by default; switch it on
  under **Filters → Channels** while you're saving up for a post.

## Post languages

Every post is written in one language, and you choose which languages you read. You only receive
posts in those languages, plus posts **with no language**: a photo or a video without text. Those
reach everyone in the channel.

Available right now: **English** and **German**.

- You have to keep at least one language, otherwise nothing could reach you.
- If you only pick a language few people write in, your feed fills more slowly. The app tells you
  when that's likely.

## Post languages vs. app language

These are two separate settings:

- **Post languages** (*Filters → Languages*): which posts you receive.
- **App language** (*Settings → Language*): the language of the app's buttons and menus.

You can read posts in English and German while using the app in German, or any other
combination.

## When you post

When you write a post, you choose its channel and its language. The language decides who gets it:
only people who read that language. The app suggests one based on your text, and you can change
it. See [Writing a post](/docs/writing-a-post/).

Your trust score measures how carefully you read posts before deciding on them. It's a number
from 0 to 100, shown on your profile and in **Stats**.

## What it changes

Trust decides how far your **forwards** travel:

| Score | Band | Each forward reaches |
|---|---|---|
| 0–34 | narrower reach | 2 people |
| 35–69 | normal reach | 3 people |
| 70–100 | wider reach | 4 people |

Trust **doesn't** change how far your own posts get when you publish them. You paid for that reach
with tokens, and it's the same for everyone.

## Where it starts

Every account starts at **50**, in the middle of the normal band. We don't know anything about you
yet, and not knowing counts neither for nor against you.

## What goes into it

- **[Attention checks](/docs/attention-checks/).** The biggest factor. Following the instruction
  in a check correctly raises your score; getting it wrong lowers it.
- **How much you review.** Reviewing regularly helps, up to a point. Volume alone can't get you
  into the top band.
- **How you review.** Deciding in a split second, or forwarding almost everything, suggests you
  aren't really reading. That caps how high your score can go.

Dropping a lot is fine. If the posts in your feed aren't worth passing on, dropping them is
exactly right.

## Only the last 30 days count

Your score is calculated from the **last 30 days** only. A bad week is forgotten after a month,
so you can always improve. It works the other way as well: a good score doesn't last on its own.

## Raising your score

Read the posts before you decide, and pay attention to the attention checks. That's all. There's
no quota, and no trick that beats reading carefully.

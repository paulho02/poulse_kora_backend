## The first readers

Your post goes to **3 readers** in its channel. They are picked at random from members who:

- read the language of your post (a post with no language can go to anyone in the channel),
- haven't received it yet,
- have room in their feed,
- and aren't you.

The price you paid buys this first step. Your [trust score](/docs/trust/) has no effect on it.

## Travelling on

Each of those readers forwards or drops your post. Every forward sends it to 2 to 4 more people,
depending on the forwarder's trust score. Those people decide in turn, and so on.

## When it waits

If nobody who could receive your post has room in their feed right now, your post waits and is
tried again regularly. It keeps looking for readers **for up to 10 days**. In a small or
new channel this can take a while, but the post isn't lost.

## When the journey ends

A post stops travelling when:

- everyone who received it has dropped it, or
- there's no one left in the channel who reads its language and hasn't had it yet.

That's the normal end of every post, not a failure. A post that reached everyone who could get it
has done everything it could.

## Following your post

**Profile → My Posts** lists everything you've published. How often a post has been forwarded
isn't shown on the post itself, not even to you. Readers only learn it after they've decided, so
that nobody simply votes with the crowd. The **Stats** tab shows how far posts travel across
Peerkola.

If a reader gifts you a token while forwarding your post, it lands in your balance. You aren't
told who it came from.

## Editing and deleting

Once published, a post can't be edited, and you can't delete a single post on its own. Forwarded
copies are already out of your hands anyway. The one way to remove your posts is to
[delete your account](/docs/your-data/#deleting-your-account) and choose to erase everything you
published along with it.

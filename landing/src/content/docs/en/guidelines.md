You agreed to these rules when you joined. They're repeated here so you can look them up.

## The rules

1. **Posts are public.** Anything you publish can be forwarded to any other user on the platform.
2. **Act responsibly.** No misinformation, hate speech, harassment or illegal content.
3. **Once forwarded, a post is out of your control.** Deleting it later doesn't undo what has
   already spread.
4. **Respect other people's privacy.** Don't share someone's private information without their
   consent.
5. **Breaking these rules can get your account suspended.**

## Why they matter here

In Peerkola, every forward is a person's decision. That gives everyone a bit of responsibility
for what spreads: authors for what they write, readers for what they pass on. Forwarding a post
means "this is worth reading", so treat it that way.

Anonymous posts are covered by the same rules. They're still linked to your account on our side.

## Seeing something that breaks them

Drop the post, so it doesn't travel any further through you. If it's serious, tell us: use
**Send feedback** in the app or write to the address on the [home page](/#contact).

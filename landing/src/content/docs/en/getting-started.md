Peerkola runs in the browser at [app.peerkola.com](https://app.peerkola.com). There is nothing to
install.

## Sign up with email

You need three things:

- **Email address.** Used for your verification code, password resets and messages about your
  account. Other people never see it.
- **Username.** The name other people see on your posts. It is 3 to 30 characters long and may
  contain lowercase letters, numbers and `_`. Capital letters are turned into lowercase, so `Anna`
  becomes `anna`. Every username exists only once.
- **Password.** At least 10 characters, using at least three of: lowercase letters, uppercase
  letters, numbers, symbols.

## Verify your email

After you sign up, we email you a 6-digit code. Enter it in the app to continue. The code is valid
for 15 minutes. If it doesn't arrive, check your spam folder, then tap **Resend** once the
countdown has run out.

You can't read or publish posts until your address is verified.

## Sign up with Google

If the sign-in screen offers **Continue with Google**, you can use a Google account instead of a
password. We suggest a username based on your Google account, and you can change it before you
continue. Only Google accounts with a confirmed email address are accepted.

If you already have a Peerkola account with a password, see
[Signing in with Google](/docs/account/#signing-in-with-google) before you link it. Linking can't
be undone.

## The first setup

Once you're in, the app walks you through a few short steps:

1. **Welcome.** Three slides with the idea behind Peerkola.
2. **Guidelines.** What's expected of everyone. They're also listed under
   [Community guidelines](/docs/guidelines/).
3. **Channels.** Pick 1 to 3 channels to start with. They fill your feed. You can join more at any
   time.
4. **Languages.** Pick the languages you can read. We start with your device's language. You'll
   only be sent posts in these languages, plus posts without any text.
5. **How Peerkola works.** An optional five-step explainer. You can open it again later under
   **Settings → How Peerkola works**.

## Your starting tokens

Every new account starts with **10 tokens**, so you can publish a first post without reviewing
anything. [Tokens and prices](/docs/tokens-and-prices/) explains how it works after that.

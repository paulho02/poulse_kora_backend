The app has five tabs along the bottom of the screen.

## Feed

Your queue of posts waiting for a decision. The tab's icon shows how many are waiting. Read a
post, then forward or drop it. See [Your feed](/docs/feed/).

## Filters

What reaches you. It has two tabs:

- **Channels:** browse and search channels, join or leave them.
- **Languages:** the languages you read. Only posts in these languages reach you.

See [Channels and languages](/docs/channels-and-languages/).

## Create

Write a new post: text, photos and videos, a channel and a language. The composer shows your token
balance and what the post will cost. See [Writing a post](/docs/writing-a-post/).

## Stats

Your activity this week, your trust score, badges, and how far posts get forwarded across
Peerkola.

## Profile

Your username, profile picture and trust score, and:

- **My Posts:** everything you've published.
- **My Reviews:** every post you've forwarded or dropped, so you can find one again. Both lists
  can be searched and browsed by date.
- **Settings:** app language, password, Google sign-in, data export, account deletion and the
  explainers. See [Account settings](/docs/account/).
- **Dark mode** and **Sign out**.
- **Send feedback:** report a problem or suggest something. See [Troubleshooting](/docs/faq/).

## Tips

The first time you open some screens, a short tip appears at the top. Dismiss it once you've read
it. **Settings → Reset tutorial hints** brings them all back.

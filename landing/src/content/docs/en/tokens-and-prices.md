Publishing costs tokens, and you earn tokens by reviewing other people's posts. That way, everyone
who asks for attention has given some first.

## Earning tokens

| What you do | Tokens |
|---|---|
| Create an account | +10, once |
| Forward a post | +1 |
| Drop a post | +1 |
| Answer an attention check | +1 |
| Someone gifts you a token on one of your posts | +1 |
| Publish a post | minus the current price |

Tokens can't be bought, and they can't be sent to someone directly. The only way to give one away
is to [gift it](/docs/forward-and-drop/#gift-a-token) while forwarding that person's post.

## What a post costs

The price isn't fixed. It rises when a lot of people are posting at the same time and falls when
it's quiet. That way nobody can flood the feed, and posting stays cheap when there's room. Across
Peerkola, a post currently costs between **1 and 30 tokens**.

Each channel and language has its own price. Busy ones cost a bit more than quiet ones, at most
half again as much as the general price, and at least half of it. That's why the channel list can
show a range, such as *2–6*: the exact price depends on which language you post in.

## Seeing the price

- In the **composer**, a pill shows the price for the channel and language you've chosen and how
  it compares to your balance.
- The quoted price is **held for a short while**; the pill counts down. Publish within that time
  and you pay exactly what you were shown.
- Under **Filters → Channels** you can switch on **Channel costs** to see each channel's price
  range.

## Not enough tokens

If your balance is too low, the composer tells you how many you still need. Review a few posts in
your feed to earn them.

Posting nothing for a while is fine too. Tokens don't expire.

You'll find your account under **Profile**, and most settings under **Profile → Settings**.

## Username

Your username is what other people see on your posts, unless you post anonymously. It's 3 to 30
characters: lowercase letters, numbers and `_`. Each username exists only once.

## Profile picture

Tap your picture on the profile screen to choose, change or remove one. It's cropped to a circle,
and the circle is what others see. JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 2 MB.

Your picture appears next to your name on your posts. It isn't shown on anonymous posts.

## Password

**Settings → Change password** needs your current password and a new one: at least 10 characters,
with at least three of lowercase letters, uppercase letters, numbers and symbols.

Forgot it? See [Troubleshooting](/docs/faq/#i-forgot-my-password).

## Signing in with Google

You can switch an account that uses a password to signing in with Google instead:
**Settings → Sign in with Google instead**.

> This can't be undone. Afterwards you only sign in with Google, and your password stops working.
> You confirm with your current password.

- The Google account doesn't need to use the same email address. Your current address stays your
  contact address for messages from us.
- A Google account can only be linked to one Peerkola account.
- Linking works in the app, not in the browser version.

Accounts that sign in with Google have no password, so password changes and resets don't apply.

## Languages

- **Settings → Language:** the language of the app itself. *System default* follows your device.
- **Filters → Languages:** the languages of the posts you receive. See
  [Channels and languages](/docs/channels-and-languages/).

## Dark mode

Switch it on and off on the **Profile** screen.

## Notifications

Peerkola doesn't send notifications yet. The switches under *Notifications* in the settings are
marked *Soon* and do nothing for now.

## Explainers

- **Replay intro:** the welcome slides and guidelines again.
- **How Peerkola works:** the five-step explainer.
- **Reset tutorial hints:** brings back the tips you've dismissed.

## Download or delete

Both are under **Settings → Account** and described in [Your data](/docs/your-data/).

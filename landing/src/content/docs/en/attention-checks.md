Every now and then, a post in your feed isn't a real post but an attention check. It's how
Peerkola finds out whether people are actually reading.

## How to recognise one

- It's marked with a **small icon**. Hover or tap it for an explanation.
- It's published by the **peerkola** account.
- Its text tells you what to do: **forward** it or **drop** it.

We mark checks openly on purpose. Testing people without telling them would be a trick.

## How to answer

Read the text and do what it says. Afterwards the app tells you whether you passed.

## What it does

- Answering correctly raises your [trust score](/docs/trust/); answering wrongly lowers it.
- You earn **1 token**, as with any review.
- Nothing else. A check doesn't count as a review in your statistics, it isn't sent on to anyone,
  and forwarding it doesn't reach people.

## How often

Roughly one in every twenty reviews, and never twice in a row: at least five real posts come
between two checks. The wording changes, so you won't keep seeing the same one.

Every post in your feed waits for one of two decisions. There's no third option and no scrolling
past.

## Forward

You think the post is worth reading. It goes on to a few more readers in the same channel: people
who read its language and haven't received it yet. How many depends on your
[trust score](/docs/trust/):

| Trust score | Your forward reaches |
|---|---|
| 0–34 | 2 people |
| 35–69 | 3 people |
| 70–100 | 4 people |

A new account starts at 50, so its forwards reach 3 people.

## Drop

You don't think the post is worth passing on. It won't travel any further through you. The other
people who received it decide for themselves.

Dropping isn't a punishment or a report. It just means "not from me".

## Both earn a token

Every review, forward or drop, earns you **1 token**. Neither choice pays more than the other.
Forward what you'd want others to see, and drop the rest.

## What you find out afterwards

Once you've decided, the app shows how many times the post has been forwarded so far. You only
see this *after* deciding, so the crowd's opinion doesn't make the decision for you.

## Gift a token

When forwarding, you can choose **Gift a token** (under *More forward options*). The token your
review earns then goes to the post's author instead of you, which helps them pay for their next
post.

- The author isn't told who gifted it.
- It's a transfer, not an extra token: you give up the one you would have earned.
- It isn't possible for attention checks, or for posts whose author has deleted their account.

In **My Reviews**, these forwards are marked *token gifted*.

## Not too fast

Reviews are meant for reading. If you make a lot of decisions very quickly, the app asks you to
wait a moment before the next one. Rushing also holds your [trust score](/docs/trust/) back.

## Changing your mind

A decision can't be undone. A forwarded post is already on its way, and a dropped one has left
your feed. You'll find every review under **Profile → My Reviews**.

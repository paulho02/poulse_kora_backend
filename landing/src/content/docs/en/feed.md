Your feed is a queue of posts that have been passed to you. Nothing in it is ranked. Each post is
there because it was published in one of your channels and reached you directly, or because
someone forwarded it to you.

## A queue, not a scroll

The feed holds **up to 20 posts**. Each one waits until you forward or drop it. After that it
leaves your feed for good, and you won't be sent the same post again. You also never receive your
own posts.

You don't need to refresh. New posts show up on their own while the app is open.

## When the feed is empty

**All caught up** means you've decided on everything that reached you. That's normal and doesn't
mean something is broken. Every post currently travelling has already found its readers. More
arrive as people publish and forward.

If your feed stays empty for a long time, check:

- **Your channels.** Without a channel, nothing can reach you. Join some under
  **Filters → Channels**.
- **Your languages.** If you only read a language few people post in, fewer posts reach you. Add
  any other languages you read under **Filters → Languages**.
- **The channel filter.** See below.

## When the feed is full

Once 20 posts are waiting, nothing more fits. The notice **That's your queue for now** means:
review a few to make room. Meanwhile, posts that could have gone to you go to other readers, or
wait until someone has room.

## Filtering by channel

The row of chips at the top of the feed lists your channels. Pick one to see only its posts, or
**All** to see everything again. The filter only changes what you see; posts from the other
channels are still waiting in your queue.

## "Post unavailable"

Sometimes a post in your queue can't be shown anymore. Usually its author deleted their account
along with everything they published. Tap **Remove from feed** to free the slot. That isn't a
review and doesn't earn a token.

## Attention checks

Every now and then a post is marked with a small icon. That's an attention check, not a real post.
See [Attention checks](/docs/attention-checks/).
